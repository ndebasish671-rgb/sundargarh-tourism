About this guide

Sundargarh Tourism Guide

This is an open-source travel guide to Sundargarh district, Odisha — one of Western Odisha's most forested and mineral-rich districts, home to the Khandadhar waterfall, Rourkela's temple landmarks, hill-ringed dams and Neolithic heritage caves.

✅ How we keep details correct

Every place listing is compiled from official and reputable sources:

  • District administration — sundargarh.odisha.gov.in & sundergarh.nic.in
  • Odisha Tourism, Dept. of Tourism & Culture — dot.odisha.gov.in / odisha.gov.in
  • Odisha Eco-Tourism (nature camps) — ecotourodisha.com
  • Reputed travel references (Odisha360, OdishaTour, Wikipedia, Tripadvisor reviews)

Each detail page shows a “last verified” date and a direct link to search Google for live updates, so you can always confirm current timings and fees before travelling.

🔄 Daily sync from Google & live sources

A GitHub Actions workflow (.github/workflows/daily-sync.yml) runs once every day and updates this site automatically:

  • Google News RSS — pulls the latest headlines for Sundargarh / Rourkela / Odisha into the home page news feed
  • Open-Meteo weather API — refreshes current conditions and a 3-day forecast for Sundargarh (21.50°N, 84.03°E)
  • The workflow commits the refreshed data/news.js and data/weather.js files back to the repository, and GitHub Pages redeploys the site
ℹ️ Why RSS instead of scraping google.com? Google's search results page can't be legally/reliably scraped. Google News RSS is Google's own public feed, so the site stays compliant while still syncing fresh Google-sourced news every day.

🗺️ About Sundargarh district

Spread across Odisha's western border (~407 km from Bhubaneswar), Sundargarh is a tribal-majority district with roughly 43% forest cover, ~2 million people, and a landscape that mixes steel plants and mines with waterfalls, gorges and temples. Key towns: Rourkela (industrial hub), Sundargarh (district HQ on the river Ib), plus block towns like Rajgangpur, Kansbahal, Lephripara and Baragaon.
