How to reach Sundargarh

Sundargarh sits on Odisha's western border, ~407 km from Bhubaneswar on the Sambalpur–Rourkela highway.

✈️ By air

  • Rourkela Airport (RRK) — 6 km from city centre; limited flights to Bhubaneswar (small aircraft — check schedules)
  • Jharsuguda Airport (Veer Surendra Sai) — ~125 km; best-connected nearby airport
  • Ranchi Airport (Birsa Munda) — ~140–200 km via road
  • From any airport: hire a taxi or take buses towards Rourkela/Sundargarh

🚆 By train

  • Rourkela Junction — major railhead on the Mumbai–Kolkata line
  • Jharsuguda JN — ~35 km from Sundargarh town
  • Panposh — 5 km from Vedvyas Dham
  • 11 direct Bhubaneswar–Rourkela trains (incl. Vande Bharat ~6h40) & ~10 from Howrah (~6h)

🚌 By road

  • Regular OSRTC & private buses to Rourkela from Bhubaneswar, Sambalpur, Cuttack
  • Sundargarh town lies on the Sambalpur–Rourkela highway
  • Shared autos and taxis connect block towns (Baragaon, Lephripara, Rajgangpur…)
  • Hire local taxis for waterfall and forest destinations

🌤️ Best time to visit

  • Oct – Feb — ideal: pleasant weather, full waterfalls, picnic season
  • Jul – Sep — waterfalls at peak flow; forest trails slippery, carry caution
  • Mar – Jun — hot; prefer dams, temples and evening visits
  • Nov – Dec — peak picnic months at Darjing & river beaches
Suggested itineraries

Ready-made routes

Day 1 · Rourkela circuit

AM

Hanuman Vatika

75-ft Hanuman statue & temple garden — start early (6 AM open).

Mid

Vaishno Devi Temple

3 km away — climb ~600 steps through Durgapur hill forest.

PM

Vedvyas Dham + Pitamahal

River confluence in the afternoon, dam park for sunset.

Eve

Jagannath Temple / Indira Gandhi Park

Evening aarti, then zoo park or city shopping.

Day 2 · Waterfall & dam day

6 AM

Drive to Khandadhar

~104 km via Bonaigarh — carry breakfast; last 2 km on foot/steps.

Noon

Khandadhar viewpoint

650 steps down to the falls; picnic lunch near the base.

PM

Return via Mandira Dam

Reservoir viewpoint and boating permission (8 AM–6 PM).

Eve

Sarafgarh / Darjing option

If light remains: floating jetty at Sarafgarh or blue-stone river at Darjing.

🧳 Packing checklist

  • Waterproof shoes with grip (forest trails & waterfall steps)
  • 2 L water + snacks — services are sparse near forest sites
  • Sun cap, sunscreen, insect repellent
  • Cash — UPI may not work in remote villages
  • Light jacket (Oct–Feb) and rain gear (Jun–Sep)

⚠️ Safety notes

  • Don't attempt swims in rivers/dams — currents are deceptive
  • Forest roads: start early, avoid driving after dark
  • Monsoon: check road status before heading to Khandadhar/Miriglotah
  • Book eco-tourism nature camps in advance on the official portal
  • Respect temple photography rules and local customs
