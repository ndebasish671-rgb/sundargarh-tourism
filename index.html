Why Sundargarh

A district of waterfalls, rivers and tribal culture

💧 Waterfall country

  • Khandadhar — 244 m horsetail fall, 12th highest in India
  • Miriglotah — 30 m hidden forest cascade
  • Plus Badalgiri, Tinkuda, Bandhkaman & Khambeswari falls

🛕 Spiritual circuit

  • Vedvyas Dham — where the Mahabharata was composed
  • Hanuman Vatika — 75-ft Hanuman statue & temple garden
  • Vaishno Devi, Jagannath & Rani Sati temples in Rourkela

🏞️ Lakes & picnics

  • Mandira Dam — steel-plant reservoir amid hills
  • Sarafgarh — floating jetty & eco nature camp
  • Darjing, Deodhara & Pitamahal river picnic spots

🏛️ Heritage & nature

  • Ushakothi — Neolithic cave paintings
  • Junagarh Fort — historic fort & tantra caves
  • Ghogar Dham gorge on the river Ib
🌿 Responsible travel: Carry back your waste, respect forest & temple rules, and check with local authorities before entering forest roads during monsoon.
